What you'll actually see

Four moments worth the whole trip — not staged, not a photo filter.

Ciwidey

A Lake That Changes Colour

Kawah Putih's milky turquoise crater, formed by an old volcanic eruption — walk right up to the sulphur shoreline.

Lembang

Lunch on the Water

Floating Market's boats and bamboo rafts serve up West Javanese street food right where you're sitting.

Rancabali

370 Metres of Nerve

Rengganis Suspension Bridge — one of Southeast Asia's longest — strung across a tea-covered valley.

Bogor

Lions at Arm's Length

Drive straight through Taman Safari's open enclosures — no glass, no fences, just your car window.

Day by day

Five days, three regions — paced to give every stop real time, not a drive-by.

Day 1 — Jakarta

The Capital

Monas, Istiqlal, and the city's grand malls. Settle in before heading for the hills.

Day 2 — Lembang

Up to the Highlands

Floating Market, The Lodge Maribaya's pine forest, an evening at Rumah Mode.

Day 3 — Ciwidey → Bogor

Crater and Crossing

Kawah Putih at sunrise, Rengganis Suspension Bridge, then a scenic drive through Puncak's tea estates.

Day 4 — Bogor

Wild Java

A full day at Taman Safari, a quick stop at a local waterfall before heading back to Jakarta.

Day 5 — Jakarta

Last Stop, Souvenirs

Breakfast, a final stroll through Blok M, then off to the airport.
